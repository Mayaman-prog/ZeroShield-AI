ZeroShield AI - ZS-020 Isolation Forest PoC

Feature-schema decision:
The Isolation Forest PoC will reuse the same 71 leakage-controlled numeric DataSense features used by the XGBoost known-class PoC.

Reason:
This prevents feature-definition drift between supervised known-class evidence and anomaly/novelty evidence and improves reproducibility.

Detector semantics remain separate:
XGBoost = supervised evidence among trained classes.
Isolation Forest = anomaly/novelty evidence relative to learned reference behaviour.
Anomaly does not mean malicious and does not prove a zero-day attack.
