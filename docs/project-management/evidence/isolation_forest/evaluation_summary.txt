LIMITATIONS
Isolation Forest did not flag all attack records as anomalous.
Recon, bruteforce and MITM showed relatively low anomaly flag rates.
This demonstrates that attack traffic is not necessarily statistically anomalous relative to the benign reference.

INTERPRETATION
Isolation Forest output is anomaly / novelty evidence only.
Anomaly does not mean malicious.
The anomaly score is not a maliciousness probability.
The model does not independently prove a previously unseen or zero-day attack.
